05 // INSIGHTS

What we found

 

01

RUSH HOURS ARE BRUTAL

Speeds fall from about 39 km/h to 20 km/h in the 07–09 and 17–20 windows while volume more than doubles. Same roads, half the speed.

02

STATION ROAD IS THE CHOKEPOINT

S04 Station Road is the slowest sensor all day, followed by Market Road and College Road. These are the first places to add signal timing or lane capacity.

03

GO VIA ROUTE 3

R3 is the longest on paper but the quickest in practice at 8.3 min, because its roads keep moving. A congestion-aware router should recommend it.
